Naam Jaap Counter v2.9.12 — Manual Profile Photo Adjust

WHY THIS UPDATE
Automatic cropping cannot reliably place every person's face correctly.
This version lets the user adjust the photo before saving.

NEW PHOTO FLOW
1. Settings > Profile > Choose Photo
2. A preview/adjust panel opens.
3. Face Position slider:
   - move towards 0% to show more of the TOP of the photo
   - move towards 100% to show more of the BOTTOM
4. Zoom slider:
   - 100% = normal cover crop
   - increase only when the face is too small
5. Tap Apply Photo.
6. The final 768x768 crop is saved locally.

BENEFITS
- Full face can be centred manually.
- No more fixed auto-crop guess.
- Preview is shown before saving.
- Saved output quality: 768x768, WebP/JPEG quality 94%.
- Header and Signed-in Account use the exact same saved crop.

IMPORTANT
After installing v2.9.12, choose the ORIGINAL photo again.
The old saved photo was already cropped by the previous version and cannot recover
the missing part of the face.
