==========================================

1) In <head>, immediately after:
   <link rel="stylesheet" href="background-music.css?v=2915">

   add:
   <link rel="stylesheet" href="embedded-suite.css?v=embedded1">
   <script src="embedded-suite.js?v=embedded1" defer></script>

2) Upload / replace:
   embedded-suite.css
   embedded-suite.js
   sw.js
   manifest.json

3) If previous Phase 2/3 lines exist in index.html, remove references to:
   suite-link.js
   suite-branding.css
   suite-branding.js
   app-switcher.js

Do NOT change auth.js, api.js, config.js, trusted-device.js, app.js, history.js, reminder.js, Apps Script URL, or Google Sign-In setup.

Result: My App Suite becomes the first screen inside Naam Jaap, header ▦ reopens it, and Settings > More Apps becomes an internal switcher.
